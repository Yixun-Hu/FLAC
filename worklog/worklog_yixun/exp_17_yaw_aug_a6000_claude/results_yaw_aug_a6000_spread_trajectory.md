**Late-training mean spread (20k–40k, K=8):** P1 ΔT60 0.522 vs Yaw 0.073 (7x); P1 ΔEDT 5.631 vs Yaw 0.726 (8x).

**Reading.** P1's anisotropy GROWS with training — ΔT60 ~0.1 at 2.5k–15k,
then a monotone climb 0.26→0.90 as the model fits the canonical orientation;
ΔEDT jumps at 10k (1.1→3.4) and reaches 6.8. Yaw-Aug never develops it:
ΔT60 stays within 0.02–0.18 and ΔEDT within 0.14–1.72 across the entire
trajectory. Invariance is not learned late — it is never lost, because the
augmentation removes the canonical frame from the training distribution.

Cross-validation: the control endpoint spread at seed 42 (ΔT60 0.903/ΔEDT
6.807, K=8) matches the independent 5-seed A6-based endpoint computation
(0.897/6.738); the control's 0-degree S40000 values (T60 8.989 K=8) match
the published 5-seed P1@40k row (8.993±0.011) — protocol closes end-to-end.
