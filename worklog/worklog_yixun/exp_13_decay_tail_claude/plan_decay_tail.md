## 1. Hypothesis & design

**Hypothesis (from the exp_07/10 band analysis):** the recipe's InverseLR never decays (lr ≈ 4.8e-5 at 87.5k), so late training *orbits* — each metric touches band-best at different draws and no checkpoint co-occurs all-best. A **low-lr tail** should shrink the orbit (further tightened by EMA), freezing the model near band-center-or-better on all four metrics simultaneously.

**Single arm:** vanilla continuation of the anchor (`exp07_P1/.../epoch=19-step=87500.ckpt`, sha `bd3fc7db…`, immutable). **Treatment delivery (B1): a CHECKPOINT REWRITE, not the config** — torch serializes InverseLR's hyperparameters into the checkpoint and `load_state_dict` clobbers any new config values (measured: a naive config swap yields a no-op tail at 4.77e-5). A TDD'd experiment-local tool `src/tools/retune_lr_state.py` (strip_optimizer_state.py pattern: copy-only, never writes its input) produces `<save-dir>/retuned_from_87500.ckpt` with `lr_schedulers[0].{inv_gamma: 30000, power: 1.0}` (counters/base_lrs/warmup/final_lr untouched) AND `optimizer_states[0].param_groups[0].lr = 1.2765957446808513e-5` (so step 87,501 itself runs at the tail lr). `FLAC_AR_BVp1_dtail.json` = BVp1 semantic copy whose scheduler block is verbatim `{"type": "InverseLR", "config": {"inv_gamma": 30000, "power": 1.0, "warmup": 0.99}}` — it is the fresh-build source of truth + documentation; the run resumes FROM THE RETUNED COPY under it. **lr: 4.794633e-5 → 1.2765957e-5 at the boundary (0.266×, "quarter-lr" as a label) → 1.1764706e-5 at 97,500** (the budget end; 1.15e-5 would be 100k, never reached). No other optimizer/model/data deltas; recipe otherwise P1 verbatim (DDP 32×2×1, SyncBN-64, grad-ckpt, seed 42, env flac, wandb).

**Budget:** 10,000 steps (87.5k → 97.5k), **ckpt every 1,250**; screens per checkpoint under the pinned protocol **EMA weights, K=8, seed 42, cfg 1.0, steps 1, full 6,337/17 split, per-scene mean** (identical to the P1 comparator rows). **ETAs stated separately (B7): training ≈10.7 h; screens+confirm ≈2.4 h serialized after → results-complete ≈13.1 h from launch** (extends past the 10 h approval window; training itself completes within it — disclosed).
