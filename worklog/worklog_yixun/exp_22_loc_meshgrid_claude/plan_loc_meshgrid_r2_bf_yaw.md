- **Endpoints (named exactly as the P1 report computes them, room-first) [B1, N1]:** for arm A ∈ {BF, YAW} against P1, at K = 8:
  - Δe(A) = (1/16) Σ_r [ median_{q∈r} e_loc(A, q) − median_{q∈r} e_loc(P1, q) ] — "mean across the 16 registered rooms of the within-room median e_loc";
  - Δs(A) = (1/16) Σ_r [ mean_{q∈r} 1(e_loc(A, q) ≤ 1.0) − mean_{q∈r} 1(e_loc(P1, q) ≤ 1.0) ] — `success_raw@1.0`.
  Per-query joining is required for admission and room membership (§3 R2-A) but is NOT the reduction: no pooled median, no median of per-query differences. `success_oracle_normalized@1.0`, success@0.5, K = 1 / 4, and pooled-query values are secondary.
- **Inference procedure [B2]:** one shared room-resample index matrix drawn with the report's registered constants (`BOOTSTRAP_SEED = 20260825`, `BOOTSTRAP_N = 10000`) over the 16 rooms, reused for all four tests; each draw recomputes the paired room-delta mean from the resampled rooms. Sign convention: treatment − P1; improvement is Δe < 0 and Δs > 0. Raw p-values from bootstrap tail proportions, draws exactly equal to zero counted as crossing (conservative), no finite-sample correction (the exp_20 convention, kept): BF one-sided — p = P(draw ≥ 0) for Δe, p = P(draw ≤ 0) for Δs; YAW two-sided — p = 2·min(P(draw ≤ 0), P(draw ≥ 0)) capped at 1. Holm over the family of exactly 4 raw p-values {BF Δe, BF Δs, YAW Δe, YAW Δs}, α = 0.05; raw and adjusted p reported. 95 % percentile CIs of the same draws are reported as DESCRIPTIVE (unadjusted; not a verdict gate).
- **Verdicts (exhaustive, mutually exclusive) [B2]:** BF — SUPERIOR iff both BF tests reject after Holm (a one-sided p cannot reject with the wrong sign by construction); PARTIAL iff exactly one; NOT SUPERIOR iff neither. YAW — DIFFERENT-CONSISTENT iff both reject and the two signs agree qualitatively (both improve or both worsen); DIFFERENT-DISCORDANT iff both reject with one improving and one worsening; PARTIAL iff exactly one rejects; NOT DIFFERENT iff neither.
- **Secondary, descriptive only (no verdict):** the two-regime split (14 small/mid rooms vs Auditorium + Cafe), n_candidates ↔ e_loc correlation, K = 1 / 4, success@0.5, oracle-norm, and the per-arm census (must equal the registered totals 5,337 / 16 / 8,896,540 / 966,147 / 71,172,320).

   | tensor | candidate-dependent | angle-dependent | R2-B treatment |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `context_audio` | no | no | compute ONCE per query; never orbit-averaged |
   | `context_poses_vit` | no | yes | C4 ViT average, cached per query |
   | `context_poses` | yes (candidate `phi_ref`/`dphi` after the cylindrical conversion) | no | recompute once per candidate batch; not averaged |

   The released path runs the full conditioner once and additional rotated passes only for the ViT conditioners, averaging only those; r1's "context_audio over the orbit" and "context_poses per angle" were wrong and are withdrawn.
