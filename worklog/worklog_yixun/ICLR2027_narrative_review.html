The m=0 Problem

Reading every result in both worklogs against an ICLR 2027 submission: why the frame-averaging win cannot carry a paper on its own, and which finding buried in the physics synthesis can.

Bottom line

“FA beats vanilla at 40k” is not a paper. It reverses under a better recipe, it sits on a checkpoint-band artifact, and at matched compute it loses badly — all documented in your own records, so a reviewer will find it.

But the corpus contains a much better paper. The azimuthal-harmonic account in PHYSICS_CONSTRAINTS_synthesis_exp05_to_exp10.md is a physics-derived theory that makes a falsifiable prediction, has that prediction confirmed across two independent architecture families, correctly predicts a failed intervention, and hands you a derived fix that has never been run. That is the shape of a strong ICLR submission, and the missing experiment is roughly two GPU-days.

01 — The problem with the FA storySix reasons the 40k win will not survive review

Each of these is in your own records, which means it would have to appear in the paper's own ablations. Together they are disqualifying for a claim built on frame averaging alone.

1. The same comparison reverses sign when the baseline gets better

This is the fatal one. The identical single-delta (frame averaging vs. its vanilla twin, matched steps, matched seed, K=8) favours FA under the legacy 2-GPU recipe and favours vanilla under the exp_11 L40 recipe.

2. The 40k win point is a band artifact, and your team already said so

The learning rate never decays (InverseLR holds ≈4.8e‑5 for the whole run), so adjacent checkpoints swing about ±0.5 T60. FA's 40k reading of 8.190 sits 0.7–1.5 below every neighbour on both sides — 30k 8.858, 32.5k 9.022, 35k 9.733, 37.5k 9.176. The exp_10 A3 diagnostic already downgraded “FA wins 12/14 cells” to band parity.

3. At matched compute, FA is not competitive

Frame averaging costs 3.5× per step. Equalising compute against the 87.5k vanilla anchor puts FA at 25k steps: T60 ties (8.316 vs 8.293) but C50 is 1.077 vs 0.966, EDT 40.72 vs 35.95, R@1 4.39 vs 6.96. Not close.

4. The best model in the whole program is vanilla

P1@87.5k beats the released Table‑1 on 8/8 cells at both K. It has nothing to do with equivariance — it is batch-norm statistics, DDP and a longer budget. A paper claiming symmetry helps, whose own best checkpoint has no symmetry, is a hard sell.

5. Every arm is a single training seed

Five eval seeds control eval noise, not run-to-run training variance. The FA effect (0.4 T60) is smaller than the spread between your three vanilla @40k controls (8.048 / 8.611 / 8.993). This is the objection that sinks otherwise-fine papers.

6. The invariance FA delivers is not the invariance that matters

C₄ frame averaging is exact at 0°/90°/180°/270° and buys zero robustness to arbitrary yaw — exp_14 measures C4's degradation as statistically indistinguishable from vanilla's (p ≈ 0.5). Reviewers who know the canonicalization literature will ask this immediately.

02 — Live right nowThe experiment that decides whether FA survives at all

Running  exp_14 fa_drawshare (DS‑PA arm, ~22 h in as of this writing) is precisely the causal test of the reversal. exp_11's analysis names the prime suspect: the batched-orbit path shares one stochastic RoPE rescale draw per chunk where the legacy loop drew per angle, which reduces augmentation diversity only on the FA side. That is the single known implementation difference separating the two recipes.

So the reversal has two possible readings, and you do not yet know which:

  • Implementation artifact — chunk-shared draws crippled FA training. The FA advantage is real; exp_11's C4L was handicapped.
  • Genuine recipe interaction — FA's advantage only exists against a weakly-tuned baseline. The FA claim is dead as a headline.

It will not report in time to lead the paper. DS‑PA is at roughly 4,400 of 40,000 steps after 22 hours — about 11% — running at ~198 steps/hour under co-tenancy. That projects to ~22 Aug for arm one, and DS‑CS3 is gated behind it by design, landing near 1 Sept before any evaluation. Any pause to free GPUs pushes it further.

This is the strongest structural argument for not building the paper on frame averaging: the result that would rescue the FA claim arrives too late to be built on, and might not rescue it.

04 — InventoryFive publishable assets, ranked

A

The azimuthal-harmonic account of acoustic metrics

Strongest

This is the paper. The synthesis derives that T60 is an m=0 functional of the room — Sabine's 0.161·V/(S·ā) is rotationally averaged — so an invariant, rotation-averaged readout is not merely adequate for it but variance-reducing. C50 and EDT are not m=0: they depend on source-relative early-reflection geometry, which lives in m ≥ 1. Mean-pooling is the projection onto m=0, so it provably cannot express them.

 Under band aggregation the cylindrical arm loses all three. At matched step the EDT claim reverses. C50 is the only metric that survives every estimator — and C50 is the m ≥ 1 signature, so the harmonic account keeps exactly one empirical leg.

A cleaner demonstration already exists, recipe-matched. Within one arm family: max-pool vs mean-pool @40k K=8 gives C50 1.0592 vs 1.0811 (max better) and T60 8.896 vs 8.622 (max worse), repeated in the MLP-head pair. Escaping m=0 buys C50 and costs T60, with no cross-recipe or cross-stratum comparison anywhere. Max-pool is also exactly roll-invariant — so the competitor is max-pool, not mean-pool, and the contribution must be reframed: mean and max are lossy invariants, source-steered pooling is the complete invariant on the roll orbit.

Two further results behave exactly as the theory demands. Distillation from a task-aligned teacher failed and spent the T60 win to buy partial C50 — you cannot distil back information the readout already discarded. And the fixed-linear invariant readout probe came back uniformly negative, which is what you expect if the missing structure is nonlinear and phase-bearing.

Best of all it yields a fix that is derived rather than guessed: source-steered Fourier pooling, F̂[m]·e^{imφs}, which is exactly invariant and lossless because it retains m ≥ 1 phase relative to the source. Partial support already exists — exp_12 arm A (low-band RoPE + m0-restricted registers) beats the naive cylindrical arm on 6/6 metrics at both K, closing 68% of the T60 gap and 86% of the C50 gap to vanilla FLAC.

05 — RecommendationThe narrative I would write

Recommended title — unanimous top-1 across three judging lenses

Reverberation Time Is Rotation-Averaged, Clarity Is Not: An Azimuthal-Harmonic Account of Invariant Readouts in Room Impulse Response Generation

The main clause is a property of the acoustic functionals themselves — Sabine is a pure global average, C50 and EDT depend on source-relative early reflections — so no experimental outcome, single-seed objection or recipe reversal can falsify it. Crucially it blames invariant readouts, not equivariance: if source-steered pooling later works, an equivariant-and-accurate model would be a counterexample to any title that blamed the symmetry. “An … Account of” pre-registers the understanding-paper register with no method obligation.

Two usage notes: put yaw-equivariance in the abstract's first sentence and the keyword field, since the title carries only “invariant”; and gloss clarity (C50) and early decay time (EDT) in the opening lines so no reviewer reads “clarity” as a vague English word.

The arc has seven beats, and note that frame averaging appears as the setup and the foil, never as the contribution.

  1. Physics says the RIR field is yaw-equivariant. The models are not. Rotating the panorama by an angle that changes nothing physical moves the prediction by 0.19–0.22 relative L2 and degrades every metric by 9–23σ, worst at 180°.
  2. Both obvious fixes deliver machine-precision invariance. Frame averaging reaches 4.9e‑08 at the conditioning level; the equivariant backbone reaches 2.3e‑06. The symmetry is not in doubt anywhere in the program.
  3. And neither improves accuracy at canonical pose. Present the recipe-contingency as a controlled result rather than a caveat — this is the honest core and it is genuinely interesting, because the equivariance literature reports positive results almost uniformly.
  4. Why: the symmetry was imposed in the wrong place. Invariance was applied to the representation, by projecting it onto m=0, rather than to the prediction. T60 survives because it is m=0; C50 and EDT are destroyed because they are not.
  5. The fix follows from the diagnosis. Source-steered invariant pooling, low-band RoPE, m0-restricted registers — exactly invariant without discarding m ≥ 1.
  6. What invariance does buy is pose robustness, with the dose-response curve and the C4 trap.
  7. Practical guidance — the evaluation protocol, the band-draw warning, and the group-order choice.

If beat 5 lands, this is a positive-result paper. If it only partially lands, beats 1–4 and 6 still make a solid ICLR “empirical understanding” submission, which is a well-established genre there. Either way you are not betting the paper on a single contested delta.

Why this clears the novelty bar

Equivariance in RIR generation is essentially unexplored — a literature sweep turns up neural acoustic fields and RIR benchmarks with no symmetry treatment. Negative-and-mechanistic results on imposed equivariance do exist (2D‑to‑3D pose lifting reports the same accuracy-for-robustness trade), which helps rather than hurts: it means the framing is legible to reviewers and you are extending a recognised line into a domain where the harmonic structure gives it real explanatory teeth.

07 — ExposureWhat a reviewer will attack

  • Single training seed. The one that actually sinks papers. Addressed above.
  • “Your best model has no symmetry.” Own it in the abstract — it is the paper's point, not an embarrassment.
  • One dataset. AR only. A small HAA transfer table would disproportionately help; the finetune recipe already exists in-repo.
  • The m=1..4 variance decomposition is exploratory. 22 samples, one source index, a pretrained rather than task-trained backbone, unreviewed. It motivates the theory; it cannot be the evidence for it. Either re-run it properly at scale or present it explicitly as motivation.
  • Prior art on low-band geometry RoPE (ViewRope) needs a careful positioning paragraph.
  • Scope discipline. The corpus is enormous. Most of exp_03–06, 08, 09 and 13 will not appear at all, and trying to include them will produce an unreadable paper.
