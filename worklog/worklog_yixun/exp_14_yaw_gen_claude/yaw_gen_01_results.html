Descriptive  C8 is the efficient point at this training budget

Not pre-registered — an adjacent-contrast reading of the same data, reported as description, not as a tested claim.

  • Ties vanilla on T60 under random yaw (7.726 vs 7.724).
  • Beats it on C50 (0.868 vs 0.954, −9%) and retrieval (R@1 5.20 vs 4.44, +17%).
  • Costs EDT (+1.75 ms: 38.08 vs 36.33).
  • The fixed-order T60 chain is non-monotone (VANL→C4L worse, C4L→C8 better, C8→C16 worse, C16→C32 better): order effects mix the invariance benefit with per-arm training variance at a single training seed.

Scope and caveats

  • Single training seed (42) per arm — all cross-arm inference is conditional on these particular training runs (exp_11's standing caveat). This is the main threat to the descriptive deployment reading.
  • Matched steps (40k), not matched compute — larger orbits consumed 2–4× the training FLOPs. A matched-compute comparison is the top follow-up.
  • "Per-scene" = the release code's grouping = 10 room families (the split spans 17 physical rooms); retrieval and FD are split-level global, per the pre-registered aggregation ruling. The two co-primaries therefore come from different aggregations, by design.
  • RIR_to_geom_R@k is confounded under rotation (its gallery embeds the rotated point cloud) — quarantined to a descriptive block in the full report, never a headline or a co-primary.
  • Random yaw is uniform over the 512 exact panorama columns; in-group draws are legitimate support mass (C32: 6.25% of draws).
